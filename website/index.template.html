Set it up before the show. Use it when it counts.

  1. Add your friends

    Scan each other's QR code when you're together, or send an invite link to anyone, in any chat app. Nobody is added until you accept. Only friends can see you or message you.

  2. Go to the gig

    Keep the app running in your pocket. It shares your spot with friends and stays ready on Bluetooth.

  3. Find each other

    Open Find to see everyone on the map, drop a meeting point, or press Help.

Install it on Android

  1. Download MoshGrid ({{SIZE_ARM}}). It runs on nearly every Android phone from the last eight years. If it won't install, get the version for all phones ({{SIZE_UNI}}).
  2. Open the file. Android asks you to allow installs from your browser or Files app: allow it for this install.
  3. Open MoshGrid, enter your name, and allow Bluetooth, location and notifications. It needs all three to find friends and to alert you.
  4. Add your friends before the show: scan each other's QR codes, or send them your invite link from the Friends page.

Good to know before you rely on it

  • Android only for now. Friends on iPhone can't join yet.
  • Bluetooth has a short reach. Roughly across a large room between two phones. Messages go further when other MoshGrid phones are in between.
  • Everyone sets it up before the gig. You can only reach friends who installed the app and added you, by QR code or invite link.
  • Over the internet, a relay server passes messages on. It can't read them, but like any server it sees your phone's IP address.
  • Crash reports are on, and one switch turns them off. You see the switch during setup, before anything is sent. They go to MoshGrid’s own server, with no location, messages, names or friends in them.
  • It is not an emergency service. In danger, find venue staff or call 112.

Free and open source
